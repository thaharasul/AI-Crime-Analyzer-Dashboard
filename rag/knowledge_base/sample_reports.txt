DISTRICT CRIME SUMMARY REPORTS - ANALYST NOTES

ZONE 4 - QUARTERLY NOTE (ROBBERY TREND)
Zone 4 has shown a noticeable rise in robbery incidents over the last
two quarters, concentrated around the Central Market and MG Road
Junction areas during evening hours (18:00-22:00). Contributing factors
identified by field officers include reduced street lighting near the
market's east entrance and a high footfall of cash-carrying vendors
after the evening market closes. Patrol frequency in this corridor was
increased by roughly 30% following the second-quarter review, and
preliminary indicators suggest a slight decline in the most recent
month, though a full quarter of data is needed to confirm the trend
has reversed.

CYBERCRIME - YEAR-OVER-YEAR COMPARISON
Cybercrime reports, particularly Online Fraud and Phishing, increased
substantially year-over-year, consistent with national trends
following wider adoption of digital payment platforms. Identity Theft
cases involving impersonation on messaging platforms were the
fastest-growing sub-category. Investigators note that a large share of
these cases originate from outside the immediate jurisdiction, which
complicates enforcement and increases average case resolution time
compared to physical crime categories.

ZONE 1 & ZONE 2 - BURGLARY PATTERN
Burglary incidents in residential pockets of Zone 1 and Zone 2 cluster
heavily on weekdays between 10:00 and 16:00, correlating with periods
when residential properties are more likely to be unoccupied due to
work schedules. Repeat-offense analysis suggests a small number of
organized groups are responsible for a disproportionate share of these
incidents, based on similarities in entry method and stolen item
profile.

NARCOTICS - HOTSPOT NOTE
Drug possession and trafficking incidents show geographic clustering
near transit hubs, particularly bus depots and the railway station
corridor. Joint operations with narcotics control units have been most
effective when combined with CCTV coverage expansion in these specific
corridors rather than broad city-wide sweeps.

GENERAL RECOMMENDATION - PATROL & CCTV DEPLOYMENT
Across zones, incidents involving weapon use are disproportionately
concentrated in a small number of recurring locations. Analysts
recommend prioritizing CCTV deployment and visible patrol presence at
these recurring high-severity locations over uniform city-wide
distribution, since concentrated deployment has historically shown a
larger measurable impact on incident rates in similar-sized
districts.
