IPC SECTIONS REFERENCE - COMMON CRIME CATEGORIES

Section 302 - Punishment for Murder
Whoever commits murder shall be punished with death or imprisonment for
life, and shall also be liable to a fine. Applies to Violent Crime /
Homicide cases.

Section 307 - Attempt to Murder
Whoever does any act with the intention or knowledge that, if it caused
death, would amount to murder, shall be punished with imprisonment for
a term which may extend to ten years, and shall also be liable to a
fine. Commonly cited in Violent Crime / Assault cases involving intent
to kill.

Section 323 - Punishment for Voluntarily Causing Hurt
Applies to general Assault cases where the injury does not amount to
grievous hurt. Punishable with imprisonment up to one year, or fine, or
both.

Section 379 - Punishment for Theft
Whoever commits theft shall be punished with imprisonment of either
description for a term which may extend to three years, or with fine,
or both. Applies to Pickpocketing, Shoplifting, and general Theft
category incidents.

Section 380 - Theft in Dwelling House
Theft committed in any building, tent, or vessel used as a human
dwelling is punishable with imprisonment up to seven years. This is the
primary section applied to Burglary cases.

Section 392 - Punishment for Robbery
Whoever commits robbery shall be punished with rigorous imprisonment
for a term which may extend to ten years. If the robbery is committed
on a highway between sunset and sunrise, the imprisonment may extend to
fourteen years. Applies to Robbery incidents, especially those
involving weapons.

Section 411 - Dishonestly Receiving Stolen Property
Applies to cases involving Vehicle Theft rings and resale of stolen
goods, where a person knowingly receives or retains stolen property.

Section 420 - Cheating and Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property
Frequently applied to Online Fraud and Cybercrime cases where a victim
is deceived into transferring money or property.

Section 66C / 66D - Information Technology Act (Identity Theft and
Cheating by Personation using Computer Resource)
Applied alongside IPC 420 in Phishing and Identity Theft cases
involving digital impersonation.

Section 497 / Domestic Violence Act, 2005
Domestic Violence cases are typically handled under the Protection of
Women from Domestic Violence Act in conjunction with relevant IPC
assault sections, depending on severity.

Section 447 - Punishment for Criminal Trespass
Whoever commits criminal trespass shall be punished with imprisonment
of either description for a term which may extend to three months, or
with fine, or both. Applies to Trespassing incidents under Public
Order.

Section 425 / 426 - Mischief
Applies to Vandalism cases; whoever commits mischief causing damage to
property shall be punished with imprisonment up to three months, or
fine, or both, depending on the value of damage.

NDPS Act, 1985 - Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act
Governs Drug Possession and Drug Trafficking cases. Punishment scales
with the quantity involved: small quantity, commercial quantity, and
intermediate quantity each carry different sentencing bands, ranging
from six months to over ten years for commercial quantities.
